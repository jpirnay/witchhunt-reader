Home Screen

Choose the rows of the Lyra Tiles Home screen: the book you are reading, shortcuts, a clock, the calendar, your week, the weather and pictures. The reader shows them when its Home screen layout is Custom; saving here sets that.

    Up to six rows, each one widget across or two side by side; the book card and Also reading take a row alone. More and Settings always close the screen, and More holds every place your shortcuts leave out.
