What a full install keeps and what it loses

Kept: everything on the SD card. Your books, reading progress, bookmarks, settings, Wi-Fi networks, fonts, dictionaries, language and hyphenation packs and reading statistics all live there.

Lost from the device's own memory:

  • the clock's last known time and its learned drift: a device without a clock chip shows no time until it next syncs it over Wi-Fi;
  • which model an X3/X4 is: it is detected again at the first start;
  • the boot diagnostics counters;
  • copies the firmware rebuilds by itself: the power-button settings and time zone (from the settings on the card), the radio calibration, and the font, language and hyphenation caches, rebuilt from the card when they are first needed (the first book or language switch may take a little longer).

On a device with its original firmware (for example a new X4 Pro), a full install replaces that firmware, and what it kept in the device's memory, such as its Wi-Fi passwords, is lost with it. There is no way back unless you have a backup of the whole flash. If you may want to return to it, make one first, for example with esptool: esptool read-flash 0 ALL backup.bin.

Leftovers. Without Erase the whole flash first, parts of the flash that Witch Reader has not used yet keep their old bytes until it does; it never reads them as its own data. On a device that had its original firmware, that can include what the old firmware stored. Erase first if you pass the device on.

Update only keeps all of the above; it replaces the firmware and makes the device start it.

If something goes wrong

The device does not come back after writing. Unplug it, then press the power button (hold it for a second or two) to wake it; on an X3 or X4, press and release Reset, then quickly press and hold Power. A stock X4 Pro has once needed exactly this after a reset. Then press Pick the device again to install the web interface, or skip it: the pages install themselves later.

The page cannot reach the device, or it refuses to be written. Some X4 Pro units are locked and only take updates from the SD card. Then download the firmware image below, copy it to the card's top folder and install it on the device: on the original firmware under Settings > System > System Update > SD Card Firmware Update, on Witch Reader under Settings > System > SD Firmware Update.

Connect finds no port. Use a data cable, wake the device, and close any other program that uses the port (a serial monitor, another flasher tab).

A BOOX Picco on its original firmware shows up as a USB drive with no port. Hold Power and Volume Up (+) together for more than 10 seconds: it restarts into Onyx's recovery, which has the port. Back up the whole flash before the first install; Onyx's firmware cannot be downloaded anywhere.
